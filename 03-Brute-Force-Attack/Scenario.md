# Business Scenario

ABC Technologies has received multiple authentication alerts from a Windows workstation used by the Finance Department.

Several failed login attempts were detected within a short period.

The SOC suspects a brute-force attack against a local Windows account.

As a Tier-1 SOC Analyst, investigate the authentication activity and determine:

- Which account was targeted?
- How many failed login attempts occurred?
- Was there a successful login?
- What should the SOC recommend?
