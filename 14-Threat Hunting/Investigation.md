# Investigation

## Investigation Methodology

The investigation follows a simple SOC workflow:

1. Identify suspicious activity.
2. Identify the user.
3. Identify the process.
4. Examine the parent process.
5. Examine the command line.
6. Correlate authentication events.
7. Build a timeline.
8. Determine whether the hypothesis is supported.

---

## Hunt 1 – PowerShell

```spl
index=main EventCode=4688
| where like(lower(New_Process_Name),"%powershell.exe%")
    OR like(lower(Process_Command_Line),"%powershell%")
| table _time host Account_Name New_Process_Name Creator_Process_Name Process_Command_Line
| sort _time

Investigate:

Execution time

PowerShell process

Hunt 2 – Discovery
index=main EventCode=4688
| where like(lower(Process_Command_Line),"%whoami%")
    OR like(lower(Process_Command_Line),"%systeminfo%")
    OR like(lower(Process_Command_Line),"%ipconfig%")
    OR like(lower(Process_Command_Line),"%arp -a%")
    OR like(lower(Process_Command_Line),"%route print%")
    OR like(lower(Process_Command_Line),"%tasklist%")
| table _time host Account_Name New_Process_Name Creator_Process_Name Process_Command_Line
| sort _time

The analyst determines whether the activity represents normal administration or suspicious reconnaissance.

Hunt 3 – Authentication Correlation
index=main (EventCode=4624 OR EventCode=4625 OR EventCode=4688)
| eval Event_Type=case(
    EventCode=4625,"Failed Login",
    EventCode=4624,"Successful Login",
    EventCode=4688,"Process Creation"
)
| table _time EventCode Event_Type host Account_Name Source_Network_Address New_Process_Name Creator_Process_Name Process_Command_Line
| sort _time
Investigation Questions
Were there failed authentication attempts?
Was there a successful authentication?
Which account was involved?
What process was launched afterward?
Was PowerShell involved?
Was discovery activity observed?
Was the activity generated by the laboratory simulation?
Investigation Conclusion

The analyst should classify the hypothesis based only on the collected evidence.

Possible conclusions:
Hypothesis Supported

The collected telemetry contains activity consistent with the hunting hypothesis and requires further investigation.

Hypothesis Not Supported

The expected suspicious sequence was not observed in the available telemetry.

Requires Further Investigation

Suspicious activity was identified, but available telemetry is insufficient to confirm compromise.

The final report should use the conclusion that matches the actual Splunk evidence.
