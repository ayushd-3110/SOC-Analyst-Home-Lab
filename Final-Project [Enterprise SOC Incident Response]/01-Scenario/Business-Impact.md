# Business Impact

## Real-World Context

If the same sequence occurred on a production workstation, the SOC would need to consider possible:

- unauthorized account use
- endpoint compromise
- credential exposure
- lateral movement
- persistence
- data or service impact

## Lab Impact

In this project the activity was intentionally generated inside a controlled environment.

No production system, customer account, real malware, or real business data was involved.

## Why the Exercise Matters

The important skill is the investigation process:

**Alert → Evidence → Correlation → Decision → Response → Documentation**

The project shows how an analyst can investigate a suspicious chain without automatically declaring a compromise before the evidence supports that conclusion.
