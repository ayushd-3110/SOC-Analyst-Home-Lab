# Business Scenario

ABC Technologies has over 600 employees.

The SOC receives an alert indicating that one workstation is attempting connections to multiple TCP ports on another internal workstation.

Reconnaissance activity is often the first stage of an attack.

As a Tier-1 SOC Analyst, investigate the activity and determine:

- Source IP
- Destination IP
- Ports Scanned
- Time
- Process Responsible
- Severity
- Recommendation
